## Prompt

Generate a production website background asset only, landscape 16:9, ideally 1920x1080. Reference image is mood and background composition reference ONLY. Create the empty environment behind this portfolio hero: cinematic midnight black and deep navy rocky shoreline, wet black basalt rocks rising from lower left and lower right edges, a very low calm water horizon across bottom 12%, subtle electric blue reflections on water. Upper 85% is spacious very dark blue-black atmospheric sky with subtle out-of-focus blue starlight and delicate nebula haze concentrated around x=30%, y=45%, while right half stays very dark and quiet for readable white text. Rocks stay low and never block the content area. Photorealistic rock textures, elegant restrained sci-fi lighting, near-black edges for seamless fade into black website section below. Absolutely NO people, NO face, NO logos, NO icons, NO rings/orbits, NO text, NO letters, NO interface. The result will be used as a CSS background with real HTML content over it. Match the blue-black atmosphere and rocky sea bottom from the reference, without copying its foreground content.
